How to Play

★ Basics
Swap adjacent gems to match 3+ of the same type. Matches disappear & new gems fall in. Reach the score target to level up!

★ Gem Types
There are 5 gem types, each with a unique shape and color. Match 3+ of the same type to clear them!

★ Hyper-Gems
Match 4 → Line-Clear Hyper-Gem (wipes a full row/column).
Match 5 → Color-Burst Hyper-Gem (clears every gem of that color).

★ Hint Button 💡
Stuck? Tap the lightbulb to highlight a good move. Use it wisely — it helps you find the best swap!

★ No Moves?
If no swaps are possible, the game ends — plan ahead!
